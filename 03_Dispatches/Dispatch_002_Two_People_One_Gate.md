# DISPATCH_002: TWO PEOPLE. ONE GATE.

## THE PARADOX
Two people arrive at the same gate at the same time.

Person A: Laptop + WIFI. Asks "Where are the gigs?"
Person B: No 4G Device. Asks "How can I get a device?"

Same country. Same internet economy.
Different results.

Why?

## A WORKING MODEL
A useful way to think about outcomes is:
Outcome ≈ [Opportunity × Demand × Knowledge × Trust × Compliance − Cost ^ Time]
          × HUMAN FACTOR
          × CONDITIONS FIT

Note: This is a mental model, not a math formula. It's for diagnosing, not predicting.

## THE TWO GATES

GATE 1: URGENCY
When your conditions are:
- Runway < 30 days
- Capital < Ksh 10,000  
- Need cash now
Playbook: Do tasks today. Get paid this week. Build runway.

GATE 2: DELAYED GRATIFICATION
When your conditions are:
- Runway > 90 days
- Capital OR Skills to invest
- Can wait 90-365 days
Playbook: Join communities. Learn daily. Build assets that compound.

Neither gate is superior. The right gate depends on your current conditions, not someone else's journey.
WARNING: Picking a gate that doesn't match your current conditions greatly reduces your chances of success.

## THE HUMAN FACTOR
Six Human Capitals that strengthen the probability of positive outcomes:
1. Discipline: Tame greed
2. Curiosity: Ask when you feel left out
3. Consistency: Trade daily. Post daily.
4. Resilience: Start from 0
5. Community: "Lone ranger doesn't work"
6. Humility: "Someone knows something I don't"

## THE AUDIT
Before you pick a gate, audit yourself:
1. Conditions Check: What's my runway? What's my capital?
2. Gate Selection: Am I in Urgency or Delayed?
3. Human Factor Check: Which of the 6 capitals will I train this week?

Reply with: "URGENCY" or "DELAYED" + 1 capital you'll train.
