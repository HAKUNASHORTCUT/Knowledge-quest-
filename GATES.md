# The Seven Gates

The structure of the Infrastructure Handbook. Each Gate represents a domain of invisible infrastructure.

## Gate 0: Learning to See
Learn to recognize invisible infrastructure.

## Gate 1: Identity
Who validates that you exist?

## Gate 2: Payments
How does value move?

## Gate 3: Ownership
What do you truly control?

## Gate 4: Distribution
How does your work reach the world?

## Gate 5: Command Centre
How do you build resilient systems?

## Gate 6: AI & Automation
Where is intelligence leveraged, and where does dependency emerge?

## Gate 7: Stewardship
How do you leave systems stronger than you found them?
