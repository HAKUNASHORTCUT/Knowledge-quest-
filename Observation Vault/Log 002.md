# Log_002: Daily Payment Psychology Used for Spending, Not Saving

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Conversation with bodaboda rider in Nairobi. Asked why he uses M-KOPA instead of saving for a phone. 
Response: "Naeza afford daily kulipia 50 bob lkn 10,000 siezi pata na siku moja." 
Translation: "I can afford to pay 50 bob daily but I can't get 10,000 in one day."
Observation: M-KOPA, Spiro, and other asset financing companies succeed because daily payments of 50-200 KES feel affordable. 
Proposed idea: Use the same daily-payment psychology to force savings instead of spending. Phone lock as accountability if daily saving target not met.

### Why did it matter?
Reveals a mechanism: "daily micro-payments feel invisible" drives mass adoption of credit. 
The barrier to saving is not lack of accounts. M-Shwari, banks, SACCOs exist. 
The barrier is lump-sum payments vs daily affordability. 
This suggests financial products are built for disciplined savers, not for how ordinary Kenyans actually spend.
