# Log_003: Ownership Without Portability is Incomplete

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Thought experiment on digital assets: If platform disappeared tomorrow, what would move? 
Articles yes. Archive mostly. Audience some. Systems no. 
Realization: Ownership = "Can someone take this from me?" 
Portability = "Can I take it with me?" 
External signal: Substack creators now asking "Can I take my subscribers elsewhere?" 
Flagged as "100% AI generated" despite being original work.

### Why did it matter?
Reveals new layer beyond ownership. An asset can be legally yours but trapped on borrowed ground. 
Portability requires 3 things: export, compatibility, continued function. 
Shows platform incentives vs archive incentives. Platforms optimize for engagement. Standards optimize for interoperability.
Suggests next decade will reward operators who can move work/audience between ecosystems.
