# Log_005: The 5 Layers of Digital Opportunity - Not Everyone Stands at the Same Gate

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Two individuals seeking online income:
Person A: Has laptop + home internet. Posts "Am ready, where are the gigs?"
Person B: Still trying to upgrade to 4G device. Bottleneck is infrastructure.
Both want same outcome but blocked at different layers.
Framework emerged: 
Layer 1: Infrastructure | Layer 2: Access | Layer 3: Capability | Layer 4: Visibility | Layer 5: Opportunity
Additional observation: Different income paths have different time horizons. Gambling=Immediate/Chance, Gigs=Short/skill, Content=Medium/consistency, Business=Long/systems.

### Why did it matter?
Reveals mechanism: "Making money online" is not one problem. It's 5 gates. 
Blanket advice fails because people are blocked at different layers. 
Infrastructure precedes opportunity, but doesn't guarantee it.
Language matters: "Where are gigs?" = searching market. "How get 4G?" = searching entry.
This explains why Digital Bridge hubs must solve for Layer 1-2, not just assume Layer 3-5.
