# Log_006: The Digital Gorge - Coordinates, Gates, and Evolving Divides

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Meta-observation of the entire research thread. 
Analogy: Millions stand on one side of a digital gorge looking at opportunity. 
Each person stands at different "coordinates": economic, skills, infrastructure, networks, time horizon, risk tolerance.
Each sees a different bridge.
Secondary observation: Platform ecosystems have 3 populations - Producers, Consumers, Connectors. 
Tertiary observation: Gates evolve. From Infrastructure → Knowledge → Opportunity → Visibility → Trust → Attention.
"Zero gatekeeping" posts actually gate attention, not information.

### Why did it matter?
Reveals mechanism: The digital divide is not one gap. It is layered. 
First crossing = device/internet. Subsequent crossings = skills, trust, visibility, networks, incentives.
Proposes new framework: "The Digital Gorge" as working title. 
Shows research discipline: Same landscape, different observer coordinates = new contours on the map.
Critical insight: As technology solves one gate, scarcity moves to the next gate.
