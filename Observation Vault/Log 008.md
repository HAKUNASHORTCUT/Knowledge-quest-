# Log_008: Fieldwork Protocol - Survey the Landscape Before Building the Bridge

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Decision to conduct field study in Nyandarua before any CAPEX.
Framework: 5 Research Questions
Q1: Infrastructure - How do people access internet? Fibre vs Mobile vs Pain points
Q2: Devices - Smartphone only? Laptop? Shared? Broken?
Q3: Digital Behaviour - What do they use internet for? WhatsApp, TikTok, Jobs, Farming
Q4: Digital Pain Points - "Most frustrating thing online last month" not "Would you use hub?"
Q5: Payments - Earned online? What stopped you? What would you do with reliable internet?
Method: 20-30 conversations. Treat each as Field Observation. Example: Ol Kalou shop owner travels 8km to print.

### Why did it matter?
Reveals mechanism: Most entrepreneurs start with solution. This starts with terrain.
Core principle: Observe first. Build second. Scale third. Fieldwork = Phase 0.
Critical shift: Arriving as researcher not seller changes answers you get.
This tests Pattern_001: Which gate is actually blocked in Nyandarua? Gate 1? Gate 2? Gate 3?
Potential outcome: Biggest need may not be coworking but printing, training, gov services.
