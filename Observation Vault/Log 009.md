# Log_009: Crypto Kenya - When the Gate Moves from Information to Network

**Date**: 2026-07-28

### What happened?
Observation of Kenyan crypto Twitter discussion about "gatekeeping".
Core complaint: Jobs, Grants, Events not accessible to outsiders.
Analysis from OP: 
1. Information doesn't reach everyone due to distribution channels
2. Responsibility is on individual to join active communities - WhatsApp, private TG
3. Advantage goes to early joiners, networked people, those with positions
4. "This is an open space not a charity" - opportunities don't come to doorstep
5. Not enough public communities. Everything concentrated in private groups.
Conclusion: Gate isn't information anymore. It's network + attention + access to rooms.

### Why did it matter?
Validates Pattern_002: Gates evolve.
Yesterday's Gate: Knowledge Gate - "How does crypto work?"
Today's Gate: Attention Gate + Trust Gate - "Are you in the room where it's shared?"
Reveals Expectational Divide: People expect public X posts. Reality is private TG/WhatsApp.
Connects to Log_005 Gate 4: Visibility and Log_006: Scarce resources moved to Trust/Network.
Shows platform ecosystem split: X = public performance, TG/WhatsApp = actual distribution.
