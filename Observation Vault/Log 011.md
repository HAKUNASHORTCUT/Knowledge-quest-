# Log_011: Gate 2 Payments - Trust > Infrastructure

**Source**: r/KenyaStartups, June 2026

### What happened?
Ecommerce consultant reports: Client refused M-Pesa STK Push/API for luxury brand. 
Chose "Pay on Delivery" instead. Reason: "Taxation instability in the Country".
Business didn't want registered M-Pesa Business account. Preferred invoice + pending payment.
Consultant notes: Most requested Google service is "M-Pesa STK Push / M-Pesa API integration".

### Why did it matter?
Reveals mechanism: Gate 2 isn't technical. It's Trust + Regulatory Risk.
People have infrastructure + devices, but fear formalization.
Paradox: Everyone uses M-Pesa daily, but businesses avoid official integration.
Connects to Log_005 Gate 2: KYC, Payments. This is the compliance sub-gate.
