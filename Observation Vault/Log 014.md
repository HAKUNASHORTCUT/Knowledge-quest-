# Log_014: Gate 3 - MMFs as Habit, Not Wealth

**Source**: r/nairobitechnies, u/InevitableArm42 - Cybersecurity/Bug Bounty + Writing

### What happened?
Mid-20s Kenyan: Income unpredictable. Invests in MMFs immediately, not "when enough".
Reason: Easy access, money not locked. Goal = habit of saving + emergency fund.
Cites drivers: Cost of living up, jobs insecure, social media financial literacy, Ziidi low barrier.
Prefers anonymity.

### Why did it matter?
Validates Log_013. Mechanism confirmed: MMFs/Ziidi = liquidity + habit formation.
Not about "getting rich". About "not losing to inflation" with small, irregular income.
Shows Gate 3 shift: From asset accumulation to financial behavior.
Connects to Log_011: Gig economy income → needs instant, low-barrier rails.
