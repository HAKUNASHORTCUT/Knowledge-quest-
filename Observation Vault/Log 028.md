# Log_028: Treasury Bans Interest on Stablecoins

**Source**: Screenshot X, @Mutua.base.etn, @moneyacademyKE
**Date**: 30 July 2026

### What happened?
Kenya's Treasury has banned interest/returns on USDT, USDC or any stablecoin held on Kenyan licensed platforms.
1.  **Kenyan Licensed Platforms**: Cannot issue interest on stablecoin holdings.
2.  **Risk to Offshore CEXs**: If extended to Binance, OKX etc, features like Binance Earn will be slashed.
3.  **Rationale Cited**: "Protecting local banks". Banks offer ~3% savings vs 5-8% APR on USDT.
4.  **Exemption**: Does NOT touch decentralized DeFi like AAVE and Compound.

### Why did it matter?
This is the "bank scrutiny" + "banning companies" fear from Log_025 made real.
1.  **Removes "Saving" incentive**: People like Jossie [Field_002] who struggle to save now lose 5-8% yield option.
2.  **Widens Gap**: Pushes users either to: Banks @3% or DeFi @higher risk/complexity.
3.  **Validates Fear**: "You don't need to be a merchant" vs "we will lose benefits" debate now has policy teeth.
