# Log_029: Observation - Infrastructure Bottlenecks

**Date**: 30 July 2026
**Source**: Fieldwork + Network Lurk + Policy Signal

### Observation
The ecosystem initially appeared to be a collection of exchanges, wallets, and traders. 
Continued observation revealed that most activity converges on a handful of critical infrastructure layers. 
As complexity increases, the number of indispensable components decreases. 
Understanding these bottlenecks is more valuable than cataloguing every platform.

### Evidence from Terrain
1. **Field_001 + Field_002**: Both have Binance, ChatGPT, TikTok, M-PESA. Tools are not the gap.
    Bottleneck: Consistent Customers + Trust + Time
2. **Log_025 WhatsApp**: "Crypto si Binance na okx. It's way deeper."
    Bottleneck: Language + Knowledge Access
3. **Log_028 Policy**: Treasury cut interest on stablecoins. 
    Bottleneck: Policy + Bank Competition

### Why It Matters
We were hunting for "which app" people need.
The real map shows "which 3 planks hold the whole bridge":
1. **Demand Layer**: Predictable customers who pay on time
2. **Cost Layer**: M-PESA fees, data, taxes, compliance 
3. **Trust Layer**: Language, education, policy, no scams

Remove one plank and the whole bridge collapses. Adding 10 new exchanges does nothing.
