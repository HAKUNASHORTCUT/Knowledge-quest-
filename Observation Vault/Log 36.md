# Log_036_The_Compliance_Gap.md
**Date Logged**: 30 July 2026
**Category**: Infrastructure / Identity / Financial Access
### Observation
Kenyan crypto community shifted from "How much did you earn?" to "Can you prove who you are?"
### Economic Signal
Cost of participation shifting. New trade-off: Low payout ←→ High verification burden
### Emerging Principle
The bottleneck is rarely finding money. The bottleneck is proving you are entitled to receive it.
