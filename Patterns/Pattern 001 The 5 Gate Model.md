# Pattern_001: The 5-Gate Model of Digital Opportunity

**Date Validated**: 2026-07-28
**Source Observations**: Log_004, Log_005
**Infrastructure Layer**: Gate 5 - Command Centre
**Status**: Validated

### 1. THE PATTERN
People pursuing economic opportunity through the internet are not all facing the same problem. 
From a distance, both "Where are the gigs?" and "How do I get a 4G phone?" look like "unemployment". 
Up close, they are blocked at completely different layers of the system.

The internet economy is not one gate. It is 5 sequential gates.

### 2. THE 5 GATES
| Gate | Name | Core Question | Failure Mode | Example from Vault |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Infrastructure | Can I get online? | No device, no power, no data | Log_005: Person B needs 4G device |
| 2 | Access | Can I enter platforms? | No account, no KYC, no payment rail | Log_004: Rural hub solves this |
| 3 | Capability | Can I deliver value? | No skill, no portfolio, no tools | Log_005: Person A has laptop but no portfolio |
| 4 | Visibility | Can I be found? | No network, no reputation, no discoverability | Log_005: "Where are the gigs?" |
| 5 | Opportunity | Can I convert to income? | No clients, no trust, no repeat business | Log_004: Capability services needed |

### 3. THE MECHANISM
**Infrastructure creates possibility. It does not guarantee opportunity.**
**Capability creates opportunity. It does not guarantee visibility.**
**Visibility creates opportunity. It does not guarantee income.**

Each gate must be crossed in sequence. Skipping gates creates friction.

This is why blanket advice fails:
"Telling Person A to keep applying" = assumes Gate 3-5 problem
"Telling Person B the same" = ignores Gate 1 problem

### 4. SECONDARY MECHANISM: TIME HORIZONS
Different paths through Gate 5 have different mechanics:
- **Gambling**: Immediate, Chance-driven
- **Gig work**: Short-Medium, Skill-driven  
- **Content**: Medium-Long, Consistency-driven
- **Business**: Long, Systems-driven

Same destination. Different gate speeds.

### 5. IMPLICATIONS FOR HAKUNASHORTCUT
1. **Diagnostic First**: Any resource must start with "Which gate are you stuck at?"
2. **Digital Bridge Model**: Log_004 hub solves Gates 1-2. Most courses only solve Gates 3-5.
3. **Archive Value**: Documenting which interventions work at which gate.

### 6. PRINCIPLE CANDIDATE
`Infrastructure creates possibility. Capability creates opportunity. You cannot skip gates.`

### 7. NEXT TEST
Find observation where someone crosses from Gate 3 to Gate 4. 
What actually moved them: skill upgrade, or network introduction?
