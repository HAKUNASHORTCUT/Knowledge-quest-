# Pattern_004: The Bridge Map - Where Capital Moves in Kenya

## The 6 Community Layers
1. **Public Communities**: X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit. Easiest entry. For observation + reputation.
2. **Telegram Communities**: Most active. Binance, ETH, SOL, Devs, OTC. Low friction.
3. **WhatsApp Networks**: Hardest. Relationship-based. OTC, merchants, agents. Referral only.
4. **Developer Communities**: GDG, ETH meetups, Uni clubs. For technical rails.
5. **Startup & FinTech Communities**: Founders, investors, compliance. Partnerships live here.
6. **Private OTC Networks**: "Hidden highways". High-volume, USDT settlement. Trust + Volume needed.

## The 5 Bridge Layers - How Infrastructure Actually Ships
**Layer 1 — Builders**: Developers, GDG, ETH meetups
**Layer 2 — Liquidity**: OTC traders, market makers, Private OTC Networks
**Layer 3 — Distribution**: Freelancers, merchants, businesses, WhatsApp Networks
**Layer 4 — Infrastructure**: Payment rails, APIs, wallets, Telegram Bots
**Layer 5 — Regulation**: Compliance, licensing, banking, M-PESA integration, LinkedIn FinTech

## Key Insight
Your crypto-to-M-PESA rail is Layer 4. 
You don't sell to consumers. You sell to Layer 2 and Layer 3 so they can serve Layer 5.

Entry rule: Start at Layer 1-2, earn trust, then climb to Layer 5.
