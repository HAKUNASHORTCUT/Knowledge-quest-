# Knowledge Quest

## Motto
Observe reality. Reveal infrastructure. Preserve understanding.

## Purpose
Knowledge Quest is a living knowledge infrastructure dedicated to transforming 
everyday observations into enduring principles, practical frameworks, and institutional memory.

## How to Contribute - The Knowledge Flywheel
1.  **OBSERVE** — Capture signals in `01_Observation_Vault/`
2.  **INVESTIGATE** — Develop observations through research in `04_Research/`
3.  **SYNTHESIZE** — Extract recurring patterns and principle candidates
4.  **PUBLISH** — Share validated insights through Dispatches
5.  **STEWARD** — Preserve proven knowledge in `06_Handbook/`

## Success Metrics
Success is measured by decision quality, not attention.

## Closing
Building institutional memory, not content.
