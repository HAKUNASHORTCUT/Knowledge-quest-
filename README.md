## Purpose
Knowledge Quest is a living knowledge infrastructure dedicated to transforming everyday observations into enduring principles, practical frameworks, and institutional memory.

## How to Contribute - The Knowledge Flywheel (5 steps)
1. OBSERVE — Capture signals in `01_Observation_Vault/`
2. INVESTIGATE — Develop observations through research in `04_Research/`
3. SYNTHESIZE — Extract recurring patterns and principle candidates in `02_Patterns/`
4. PUBLISH — Share validated insights through Dispatches in `03_Dispatches/`
5. STEWARD — Preserve proven knowledge in `06_Handbook/`
