## The Rules (Canon)

**Rule #9: Movement is universal.**
> Infrastructure determines how far value can travel. Ownership determines who benefits when it arrives.
> Source: [[KQ-01: DISTRIBUTION]]

**Rule #10: Map the protocol.**
> Do not optimize for the interface. Map the protocol. The dashboard tells you what happened. The architecture determines what becomes possible.
> Source: [[KQ-02: PROTOCOL]]

**Rule #11: Plug into the six layers.**
> Compute, Payments, Identity, Distribution, Trust, Data - plug in and effort compounds. Operate outside them, you burn out against invisible friction.
> Source: [[KQ-03: LEVERAGE]]

**Rule #12: Prepare for the handoff.**
> The control layer isn't a person you can appeal to, it is a chain of automated systems, each holding only part of the picture.
> Source: [[KQ-04: CONTROL LAYER]]

### Pattern: Distribution Compounds Early
- **Where:** Log 008
- **Shape:** Stranger → Reader → Subscriber → Archive → Trust → Extended Transmission
- **Law:** No virality required. System keeps flowing.

### Pattern: Dashboard vs Protocol
- **Where:** Log 009
- **Shape:** Interface = terminal, Protocol = baggage routing / ATC / fuel
- **Law:** Updates are inevitable. Surface-dependent builders restart. Protocol-aware operators reroute.

### Pattern: Six Layers
- **Where:** Log 010
- **Stack:** Compute | Payments | Identity | Distribution | Trust | Data
- **Modes:** Tenant (~1x) → Optimized (modest) → Operator (compounding)

### Pattern: Four Switches & The Cascade
- **Where:** Log 011
- **Switches:** Identity → Liquidity → Clearing → Access
- **Friction Location:** Not inside switches, in the handoff between them
